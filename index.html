About me

I am a junior at Boston University studying both journalism and data science. I am deeply interested in the intersection between these two fields, and I hope to further hone my skills in both areas by being able to think in terms of the other.

At BU, I’ve worked on several multimedia journalism projects including producing for my campus’s morning news show The Wire as well as writing and creating social media content for the university’s award-winning student publication The Daily Free Press. My skills in broadcast coverage, writing, and investigative reporting are always being pushed through the amazing lineup of both my professors and peers here.

From studying and practicing Python, SQL, MongoDB, HTML, and CSS, I have been able to take my storytelling skills to the next level by digging deeper into the fine details of a story. Through my coursework, I’ve been able to take datasets, find the overarching trends in it, and present it both visually and through oral presentation. In the future, I hope to uncover more meaningful, data-driven stories through my expertise in data analysis.

I’m always looking to connect with other individuals in the data or journalism community. Let’s connect and try to bridge the gap between these two fields together!
